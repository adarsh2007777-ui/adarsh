---

## 🌟 Overview
**Karma Mining** (also known as the **Karma Marathon**) is a high-energy, 12-day peer learning challenge hosted by the **µLearn PRC Chapter**. Designed specifically for freshers, the event bridges the gap between academic theory and real-world technology through gamified practical tasks from the **µJourney** catalog.

---

## 🎯 Key Highlights & Structure
- **1st-Year Students Exclusive:** Welcoming freshers to the world of open source, design, programming, and community.
- **Dedicated Volunteer Mentors:** Every group of students is paired with a dedicated volunteer senior for doubt resolution, task reviews, and guidance.
- **µJourney Quests:** Choose and complete tasks across:
  - 💻 **Tech & Coding:** Web Development, Git & GitHub, Python Scripting.
  - 🎨 **Creative & Design:** Figma UI/UX, Event Posters.
  - 🌐 **Open Source:** First PRs, Documentation.
  - 🗣️ **Soft Skills:** Peer Mentoring, Tech Blogging.
- **3,000 Karma Qualification Threshold:** Minimum score required to earn the official µLearn Certificate of Mastery and contest for grand prizes.
- **Prestigious Honors:**
  - 🏆 **Top Student Topper:** Awarded for the highest individual Karma score.
  - 🎖️ **Top Volunteer Mentor:** Awarded to the mentor whose group scores the highest aggregate Karma.
