⚙️ HOW IT WORKS

Event Structure & Flow

Six foundational mechanics that make Karma Mining fair, thrilling, and profoundly rewarding for every participant.

STEP 01

Group Allocation

All registered 1st-year students will be organized into balanced peer groups to foster camaraderie, mutual problem solving, and healthy collaboration.

STEP 02

Dedicated Volunteer Mentor

Each group is paired with an experienced volunteer mentor who provides continuous support, resolves conceptual doubts, and guides task submissions.

STEP 03

µJourney Task Selection

Students choose tasks from the µJourney catalog tailored to their interests—whether beginner coding, design, open source, or communication.

STEP 04

Earn Karma Points

Upon verifying completed tasks through public links (GitHub, Figma, live demo), Karma points are credited to each student's profile.

QUALIFY

The 3,000 Karma Mark

Mandatory Benchmark: A minimum of 3,000 Karma points must be accumulated during the 12-day window to qualify for certificates and grand awards.

STEP 06

Toppers & Volunteer Glory

The student with the highest Karma becomes the Student Topper. The volunteer whose group scores the highest aggregate Karma takes the Top Volunteer Prize!

🗓️ EVENT TIMELINE

The 12-Day Challenge Journey

From 16th August to 27th August 2026. Every phase is paced to maximize learning, community bonding, and karma accumulation.

DAY 01 • AUG 16

Grand Kickoff & Group Allocation

Welcome orientation for all 1st-year participants. Groups are published, and students meet their dedicated volunteer mentor in their breakout circle.

DAY 02–04 • AUG 17–19

Foundational Quests & First 1,000 KP

Beginners dive into Git/GitHub setup, HTML/CSS portfolio starts, and platform onboarding. Volunteers host daily evening syncs for questions.

DAY 05–08 • AUG 20–23

Mid-Sprint Checkpoint & Domain Deep Dive

Students tackle higher-value quests: Python scripts, Figma UI design, and open-source contributions. Group Karma tallies begin to heat up!

DAY 09–11 • AUG 24–26

The 3,000 KP Qualification Rush

Sprint mode! Students push to surpass the 3,000 Karma threshold. Volunteers assist with submission polishing, documentation, and peer endorsements.

DAY 12 • AUG 27

Grand Finale & Submission Cutoff

Final task submissions close at 11:59 PM. The µLearn verification engine tallies all verified karma scores for students and volunteer groups.

AUG 28+ • CEREMONY

Winners Felicitation & Award Ceremony

The Top Student and Top Volunteer Group are awarded trophies, special prizes, and official honors before the entire µLearn campus community.

❓ QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything first-year students and volunteers need to know before the 12-day sprint kicks off on August 16th.

Karma Mining is exclusively curated for 1st-year students across all departments. Seniors participate as dedicated Volunteer Mentors to guide the groups throughout the 12 days.
To qualify for official certificates, badges, and the Student Topper title, participants must accumulate a minimum of 3,000 Karma points between 16th August and 27th August 2026.
The µJourney section includes practical, beginner-friendly tasks covering web development (HTML/CSS/JS), Git & GitHub fundamentals, Python scripting, Figma UI design, open source documentation, and communication skills. You can choose tasks according to your personal interest!
Each group has a designated Volunteer Mentor. Your mentor will help you navigate the µLearn platform, recommend tasks matching your goals, troubleshoot errors or doubts, and review your submissions before final verification.
The student with the highest verified Karma points by 11:59 PM on 27th August 2026 will be crowned the Student Topper. The volunteer mentor whose assigned group generates the highest overall Karma points will receive the Top Volunteer Award!
